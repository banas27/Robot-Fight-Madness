# Tracked AI evaluation protocol

Frozen physics and original policies come from `4dadb397d17ea72863883373ff8902b5898ef044`. All four pairings use the functional tracked review scene with the production solver and the original 180 Hz timestep. Standard and swapped starts share the same seeded offsets, relative facing and robot insertion order. Swapped rotates both poses by 180°. Every pairing must publish the expected policies, two belts and six road wheels. The default wheeled arena keeps both original policies.

Development uses the existing 20-seed list in [seeds.json](../riptide-ai/seeds.json). The separate [reserved manifest](seeds-reserved.json) was generated without simulating the seeds. It uses a fresh seed range, disjoint from both previous Riptide splits. The commit introducing this protocol and manifest is the candidate freeze. Run all four pairings in a clean checkout of that commit. Make no further policy changes from reserved results.

## Measurements

- Wins and draws use the production result. Health is percentage of each robot's own maximum. HP damage is actual HP lost, capped at zero HP. Damage dealt by one robot is damage received by the other.
- An attack pass starts on entry to ATTACK. Bulldozer's contiguous RAM_PRESS belongs to the same pass. The numerator counts passes containing at least one awarded damage event. Hits outside a committed pass count toward damage and hit totals. A side hit is at least 60° off the defender's nose in the defender's local space and a rear hit starts at 135°. A clash is a distinct production clash event after its existing event cooldown. Continuous contact frames are available in optional detailed traces.
- Pushing requires a forward drive order above 0.4 m/s and an actual front contact ahead of local z = −0.30 m with a contact normal opposing the nose (dot below −0.35). Productive ticks have the opponent advancing along that nose at 0.45 m/s or more. Ineffective push time is the remainder. The metric observes displacement rate during the push and does not infer which forces caused all of that motion.
- A pin requires opponent and wall contact at the same tick. A stall is at least 0.35 continuous seconds at a center gap under 3.15 m and planar speed under 0.65 m/s while contacting a body or executing an attack, reposition or recovery. Retreat time is time in RECOVER or EVADE. A recovery creates separation with at least 0.45 m gap growth, 0.35 m own travel and 0.13 seconds clear of the opponent. An exit is sustained if the next 0.60 seconds stay free of contact and retain 0.20 m gap growth. Any physical rail pin in that window counts as a re-pin. End-of-fight censoring is counted separately.
- A meaningful exchange is an awarded damage event or a productive push lasting at least 0.15 seconds. Report each fight's longest gap, including before its first exchange and after its last. A gap of five seconds or more flags a long gap.
- Retain the per-case engine logs and raw reports. Count normalization warnings and all warned fights. Sample finite published transforms, velocities, rotor speed, drive order and robot contacts at each combat tick. Flag the existing solver review thresholds for depth, prolonged depth, speed, lift, containment and floor clearance. A flagged witness or floor clearance calls for inspection and is retained in the aggregate.

## Adoption gates

Compare each isolated candidate to the baseline on the 40 reserved starts. Inspect both layouts and the combined pairing separately. A win count or the outcome on a particular seed is descriptive.

- Riptide should increase genuine side/rear hits and improve attack conversion with a mean HP damage margin at least 5 HP per fight better. Require no increase greater than 0.25 s in mean pinning, 0.5 s in mean stalls or 3 percentage points in retreat share. Sustained exits should not decline, and the mean longest quiet gap should not increase by more than 0.75 s.
- Bulldozer should improve mean HP damage margin by at least 5 HP per fight, retain attack conversion within 5 percentage points, and reduce ineffective push time or show a better productive share without adding more than 0.25 s of ineffective push per fight. The mean longest quiet gap should not increase by more than 0.75 s.
- Both require stable finite published states, presentation/logging parity, and review of any materially worse collision or floor response. A reproduced engine warning by itself does not invalidate the run. Missing or unverified results prevent an adoption recommendation. Wins with repeated stalled approaches or pressure should not outweigh a failed tactical gate.
